# HCOS™ Governship Maturity and Assessment

The HCOS™ Governship Maturity Model supports reflection and continuous improvement. It is not intended to function as a compliance badge or a claim of perfection.

## Five Maturity Levels

1. **Informal** — Practices depend largely on individuals and institutional memory.
2. **Defined** — Roles, processes, and expectations are documented.
3. **Operationalized** — Governship practices are integrated into everyday work.
4. **Learning** — Evidence, feedback, and experience guide improvement.
5. **Stewarded** — Governance and stewardship are embedded in culture and long-term continuity.

Organizations may mature differently across domains such as authority, transparency, learning, human protection, process integrity, and knowledge stewardship.
