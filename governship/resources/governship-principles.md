# HCOS™ Governship Principles

The HCOS™ Governship Principles establish the values that guide governance, stewardship, and decision-making throughout the HCOS ecosystem.

## Core Principles

1. Human Purpose
2. Human Dignity
3. Truth and Intellectual Honesty
4. Authority with Accountability
5. Responsibility with Adequate Authority
6. Transparency
7. Meaningful Participation
8. Proportionality
9. Continuous Learning
10. Stewardship
11. Process Integrity
12. Human Flourishing
