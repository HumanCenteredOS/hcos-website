# HCOS™ Decision Worksheet

## Purpose

The HCOS™ Decision Worksheet provides a structured way to document significant decisions using the HCOS Decision Approach.

Rather than focusing solely on outcomes, the worksheet encourages thoughtful analysis, fair accountability, and intentional stewardship.

It may be used for:

- Strategic decisions
- AI implementation
- Clinical governance
- Patient safety reviews
- Policy development
- Organizational change
- Operational planning
- Quality improvement
- Project governance

# Reflection

Before concluding, consider:

- Did we seek understanding before judgment?
- Does accountability appropriately match authority?
- Does this decision respect human dignity?
- Have we documented our reasoning?
- Have we considered long-term stewardship?
- Will future leaders understand why this decision was made?
