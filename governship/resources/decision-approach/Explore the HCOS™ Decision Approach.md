# Explore the HCOS™ Decision Approach

## Better Decisions Begin with Better Questions

Every organization makes decisions.

Healthy organizations also examine **how** those decisions are made.

The HCOS™ Decision Approach provides a practical way to understand situations before assigning responsibility and to improve systems through thoughtful stewardship.

Rather than asking only **"What should we do?"**, HCOS encourages organizations to ask three complementary questions.

---

# The Three Questions

## Analysis

### Why did this happen?

Before proposing solutions or assigning responsibility, seek to understand the situation.

Healthy analysis considers:

- people
- workflows
- technology
- communication
- policies
- resources
- evidence
- uncertainty

The goal is understanding—not blame.

---

## Accountability

### Who is responsible for what happens next?

Healthy accountability aligns responsibility with authority.

It asks whether people had:

- appropriate authority
- adequate information
- sufficient resources
- reasonable expectations
- meaningful support

The goal is fair accountability—not misplaced blame.

---

## Stewardship

### How should the system improve?

Stewardship looks beyond today's decision.

It asks:

- What should change?
- What should remain?
- What should be preserved?
- What have we learned?
- How can future decisions become better?

The goal is continuous improvement—not simply solving today's problem.

---

# The HCOS™ Decision Pattern

```text
                 Understand Before You Judge

              Analysis
       Why did this happen?

                    ↓

            Accountability
Who is responsible for what happens next?

                    ↓

             Stewardship
How should the system improve?
```

This pattern can be applied to healthcare, artificial intelligence, business, education, nonprofit organizations, public policy, and other human systems.

# Reflection

Think about a recent decision.

- Did the discussion begin with understanding or with judgment?
- Did accountability match authority?
- Was there an opportunity to improve the system?
- If these three questions had guided the conversation from the beginning, what might have been different?
